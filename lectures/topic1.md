## 4. DORIS (The Doppler Orbitography by Radiopositioning Integrated on Satellite)
- DORIS is a microwave tracking system and determines the satellite’s orbit and position using radio frequency signals.
- DORIS uses the Doppler effect to measure the radial velocity of satellites relative to ground beacons.
- The dual-frequency DORIS system enables estimations of the total amount of free electrons in the ionosphere. Such data formed a separate data product.

- There are two types of ground beacons:
  - One is ODB (Orbit Determination Beacon)
  - Another is Ground Location Beacons (GLB)

- DORIS data can be used :
  - To perform on-board, real-time orbit determination (autonomous navigation)
  - To support ground-segment functions such as: precise orbit determination, precise beacon positioning, atmosphere modelling, and Earth gravity modelling

## 6. InSAR (Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar)
- Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar (InSAR) is a technique that uses the principle of interference to combine two or more Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) images over the same region.

- The resulting interference patterns, known as interferograms, capture signals from Earth’s surface, facilitating the analysis of its topography or surface motion.

- Chart Datum (CD)
    - CD is the plane to which all depth and tidal height measurements are referenced (formerly known as Admiralty Datum).
    - CD is used for hydrographic surveying.
    - Adopted as the zero point for Tide Tables since 1917.
- Relationship to Tides
  - Approximately corresponds to the Lowest Astronomical Tide (LAT).
  - Chart Datum is the plane below which all depths are published on a navigational chart.
- Relationship to HKPD
  - Chart Datum is 0.146m below HKPD.
  - It is also the plane to which all tidal heights are referred.
  - So by adding the tidal height to the charted depth, the true depth of water is determined.

- water depth = charted depth + tidal height

## Height Determination Types of Height H = h + N
1. ORTHOMETRIC(H) : The distance between the geoid and a point on the Earths surface measured along the plumb line. (From Leveling)
2. GEOID(N-geoid-ellipsoid separation) : The distance along a perpendicular from the ellipsoid of reference to the geoid. (From Model) 只考虑重力和地球旋转的影响 而平均海平面要考虑风、潮汐等因素，需要长期观察取平均值 所以二者不同
3. ELLIPSOID(h) :The distance along a perpendicular from the ellipsoid to a point on the Earth’s surface. (From GNSS Observations)
